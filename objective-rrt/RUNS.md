## Lessons
- **`combo2` identity (2026-10-29):** a release build of the 06:47–06:50 source (`builds/combo2`: `OBRRT_TWOSTAR`, WaterWarp, riser scoring) is byte-identical to `builds/combo1` with all new switches off (10 M frames from power-on, plain, seed 1, 2 threads: `nodes.csv`, `tree.bin`, `events.jsonl`). The new code is inert when off.
- **Throughput limit:** wasted picks were the biggest limit on throughput, fixed by no-start decay and then pick-by-b.
- **Long-range pull:** pick-by-b and RRT\* both lose the pull of far objectives, such as climbing toward the King. That pull came from failed attempts kept for their progress.
- **Hub zones:** they absorb effort when b is uniform over zones. Value-weighting zones (v15) didn't fix it.
- **Dedupe correctness matters:**
  - dialog states needed their own cell (v9);
  - progress the place key doesn't see (boss health, holding) needed the fingerprint (v14).
- **Long jumps:** the GA finds them once the input operators can produce Z-then-A; no objective is needed.
- **Soft dedupe** (v19) beat hard dedupe by far (4 stars vs 0 on the same settings): keeping repeats at lower weight keeps states that hard dedupe wrongly threw away.
- **Big shortcuts** exist (routes walk 2–4× the straight-line distance); the route bench's `direct` method finds 6-second-faster prefixes, but splicing them breaks the rest of the route (enemy/platform timing), so the tree must re-solve after a faster prefix (v23).
- **Speed:** RRT\* makes routes faster but explores less. Route optimisation is being tested separately in the route bench (`obrrt-route`).
- **Chomp star (v32 fix):** the post worked (3 pounds free the Chomp, who breaks the gate; ~340 freed states in v30-s2), but the tree almost never went back to a freed state. Star 6's objectives were offered while the gate still stood, so their failures decayed them and their starts went to states in front of the gate; and freed states shared one 512-unit b cell with ~540 others (0.9% of BoB b picks). Fix: star 6 (and its mission) need the gate gone (`star_blocked`), and b cells include `St::level_changes()` (post depth). Resuming v30-s2 with it: star on the first try from a freed state, in 2 of 2 seeds (~2:22)
- **Caveat on `star_blocked` (2026-10-24):** blocking a star outright is only right when it truly can't be had any other way (a 100-coin star needs its coins; that's its success criterion anyway). The Chomp gate can be clipped: speedruns use a **bob-omb clip** (throw a bob-omb, regrab it on a precise frame so it shoves Mario backwards through the bars; Mario can die out of bounds and still collect the star during the death fade). v19 also clipped a wall in WF to reach a star. A hard block removes the incentive for discoveries like these, so the block should only cover the plain approach, and a clip setup (Mario holding a bob-omb near the gate) should keep the star eligible.
- **Resume (v33):** before this fix, a resume replayed every child of a state retired earlier in the replay from the root's save. Stragglers (attempts planned before a frontier advance and merged after it) were replayed wrongly, along with all their descendants. In v32-s3 the 4th-star straggler 9593 led to the 5th star, so the resume lost it. In v19-soft-1b the resume happened to stay correct
- **Ablations with 3 seeds (v35):** 500 M frames gives 1–5 stars a seed with the same settings (v32: 2/2/5/2/3/2, sd ≈ 1.2), so a 3-seed mean has a standard error of ≈ 0.7 stars. Base 8, no-fingerprint 7, decay-route 10 stars in 3 seeds are all within that. Turning the fingerprint off cost nothing measurable on the stars we get now (Chomp, WF, CCM, PSS). Decay-route didn't hurt from power-on and is the best of the three, but showing a 1-star gain would take about 10+ seeds a config
- **Pick-by-b off (v37, v38; 12 seeds, 2026-10-25):** stars 3.17 ± 0.32 vs 2.50 ± 0.43 for v35-base (6 seeds), +0.7 ± 0.5, not beyond noise, so turning it off at least didn't cost stars. Two effects are well beyond noise: no-start picks go from 1–8% to 67–95% of attempts (they cost no frames, so the frame budget doesn't suffer), and the long-range pull comes back: Boss::Hit gets 179–810 tries in 10 of 12 seeds (every pick-by-b-on seed, v35-base and v41: 15–76), with King hits in 8 of 12 (pick-by-b on: 3 of 12). Still no King star. These runs were on pre-determinism-fix binaries under varying load, so treat small differences as noise. Entry bandit, kind bandit and fingerprint-off made no star difference (v37-nopickb-bandit, v38, v41)
- **One-off unlock states get lost in crowded b cells (2026-10-24):** after the King dies, the state with the spawned star was never sampled as b (it shared the summit cell with dozens of fight states), so with pick-by-b nothing could start Star::Collect from it, the same failure as the freed-Chomp states before v32. Fixed for the King by staging Boss::Hit through to the star; a generic fix (`OBRRT_NEW_ELIGIBLE`, boost states that can start an objective their parent couldn't) is being tested. Also: `hp.py`-style counts must split added nodes by their `success` flag, since nodes are also kept for progress alone
- **Determinism (fixed 2026-10-24, det-*):** before, `tune()` ran on the 30 s wall-clock report, so a tree depended on machine load (same binary and seed: 124 vs 117 nodes). Decisions must depend only on frames, rounds and seeded RNGs, never on wall time, RSS, thread scheduling or shared mutable state in objectives (an objective is shared by all workers). Since the fix, 2 and 4 threads gave identical trees too; earlier notes that 4 and 16 threads differ predate it. Keep `OBRRT_MAX_FRAMES` below the `minutes` limit so wall time never ends a run. Earlier note: a run is reproducible for a given binary, seed and thread count, but 4 and 16 threads give different trees. Identity checks must use the same thread count
- **Graft/replay screens (2026-10-29):** at 100 M none of the 11 graft-screen arms got past 1 star, so the graft key is not what limits early progress: lg, bnone and bstar had the fastest first stars (1:55.3–1:55.5 vs 2:03.6 off) and lg / bnone the largest replay savings (13–14 M frames), but blen and bprec got no star at all with the largest trees (65–75k states). One seed, so shortlist only
- **Machine reset (2026-10-29 ~08:50 UTC):** every lane was killed mid-run and restarted as lane_a4 / lane_b3. mx-mg-lg-s1 (cut at 172 M, 2 stars) and pk5-ps-s2 (cut at 370 M) are re-running; don't compare their partial numbers with completed 500 M runs
- **Queue fix (2026-10-29 12:50):** lane_b4 waited on PID 7801, a short-lived child of lane_b3 (PID 7799), so it started at 10:31 alongside lane_b3 and lane_a4: 32 search threads on 16 cores until 12:50. Results stay valid (runs are deterministic; ps-walg s2 re-ran identically), only slower. Cancelled kn5 (old cd base, no moves) and the rest of mx (mgel-lg s3, mg-lg); lane_a5 replaced by lane_a6 (same combo arms, 8 threads); new lane_b5 (after lane_b4). Lanes now wait on the PID of the lane script, never a child
- **Indefinite run (2026-10-29 ~13:50, user request):** every lane cancelled (ts s2–s3, cc combo arms, rr screen, pf-hier never ran; partial runs/ts-pf-ts-cb-s2 and runs/cc-combo-schema-dense-s1 are cut, don't use them). Started `runs/indef-mgel-lg-pf-s2`: mgel-lg-pf, seed 2 (7 stars at 500 M), builds/combo1, 16 threads, no frame cap (tools/queues/indef_mgel_lg_pf.sh, log logs/indef-mgel-lg-pf-s2.log). Its first 500 M should repeat pf-mgel-lg-pf-s2 exactly. Compare with indef-v47-combo (4 stars at 0.5 G, 11 at 25.7 G / 20 h)
