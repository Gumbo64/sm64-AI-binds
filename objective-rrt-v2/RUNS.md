# objective-rrt-v2: run log

v2 = v1's engine and open-loop GA, with per-state ETA objectives (GOALS.md) and a fitness of discounted ETA decrease (γ 0.99 per frame). v1's log is ../objective-rrt/RUNS.md.

- **Times** are game time from power-on, at 30 frames/s.
- **Screens:**
  - `tools/screen.sh BIN FRAMES PREFIX name="ENV" ... -- seeds` runs 2 seeds in parallel at 8 threads each, on v1's best-config base (soft dedup, `BASE`, `CD` as in v1's tools/arm_eval.sh).
  - The per-run star times come from `tools/v2sum.py`, the milestones from `tools/compare.py`.
- **`P`** is v1's best arm, mgel-lg-pf: `OBRRT_MOVES=1 OBRRT_MOVE_GA=1 OBRRT_MOVE_GA_EXTRA=1 OBRRT_CALM=1 OBRRT_LAKITU_ARC=1 OBRRT_PAINTING_FAST=1 OBRRT_ADD=last_gen OBRRT_KINSHIP_MULT=10`.
- **v1 reference:** `../objective-rrt/runs/pf-mgel-lg-pf-s{1,2,3}` at 500 M: 6, 7, 5 stars; first star 2:03.2, 2:11.5, 2:15.6.
