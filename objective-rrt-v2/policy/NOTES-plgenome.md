# plga: a GA whose genome is always a list of policies

Files: `src/plga.rs`, `src/bin/plga.rs`, `src/eval.rs` (new `play_segs`, `Drv`, `Seg`; `play_policy` now calls it), `runs/plga/` (queue.sh, bin/plga frozen binary, res/<set>/plga.{tsv,log}, genomes.txt = best policy lists, compare.py, RESULTS_plga.md = full paired tables).

## Genome and operators
Genome = ordered list `[(driver, hold frames), ...]`. Driver = a library policy with its lambda args + RNG seed (`Instance`), or pink noise from a seed (one more policy kind). The only switch rule is the hold (the last segment holds to the horizon; "policy reports done" is not implemented, off). Fitness = one closed-loop rollout from the start state (`eval::play_segs`: each policy reads the live game every frame; same Task/Roll/score as hybrid.rs; emulator frames counted exactly, incl. MPC frames). The genome is never converted to pads during search; `rollout(.., keep=true)` flattens only for the CLEAN_DUMP export. Not implemented: a recorded-pad-slice kind (it is the flattening the brief wants to avoid; pink covers raw input) and the optional MPC wrap (MPC-tagged policies can sit in the pool but cost ~14k frames per rollout).
Operators (all on the list): swap (policy chosen by per-start weights), resample args, perturb args, shift a boundary (hold +-1..12), insert, delete, duplicate, crossover at segment boundaries (cut in B nearest in time to the cut in A), pink reseed, handoff (keep the list to a random time, from a parent or a fresh pink prefix, then a pool policy takes over: the list-level analogue of `repolicy`). Operator credit = hybrid.rs market (score gain per frame, EMA, 4 % floor); pool weights start from a per-start probe of each policy alone and are updated by credit in the run. Pop 16, steady state, gen 0 = the 12 pool policies alone + pink-prefix-then-policy lists. Pool = the same 12 candidates as hybrid (default_cands), library = runs/clean/lib. 3 seeds, 20k/60k/200k/560k emu frames per start, same case files as the clean baselines. I did only 3 quick tuning iterations (handoff, pink-prefix handoff, more pink inserts) on seed 1 of `fresh` at 20k/60k; no other tuning.

## Adaptivity property (verified: `plga <rom> speed adapt`)
Same later segment B (ground-m02-gate x40) after 5 different earlier segments A (3 policies, 2 pink seeds), 6 starts: B emitted different pads in 46/53 pairs of runs (typically 35-40 of 40 frames differ); B's closed-loop pads differ from replaying another run's recorded B pads in 16/24 cases; the same genome twice gives identical pads (deterministic). So a changed early segment changes the state and later policies re-react. (Pairs that match are cases where two prefixes left the same/dead state.)

## Reading, honestly
1. The pure policy-list genome beats the equal-machinery pink GA by a large margin and reaches it at 20k-560k frames what pink needs ~1M+ for, with shorter routes than every baseline except the overlay GA at 1M frames.
2. It does NOT beat the flatten-based hybrid on success: it is far behind at 20k-60k on fresh and noise (-25 / -13 pts at 20k), and converges to within about 2-6 pts by 560k (noise within CI, fresh -5.6 with CI barely excluding 0). On field it ties (only 7 of 45 starts are solvable by this family).
3. Why it is slower early: hybrid's repolicy children inherit a parent's realised pads, then edit them with fine pad operators (jitter, button spans, drop/insert), which are cheap local refinements of an already-good trajectory; the list genome has only coarse knobs (swap, args, hold shifts, short pink segments), so local fine-tuning needs many more rollouts. Its advantage is the opposite: recombination is by behaviour, and the routes it finds are shorter (the 120 vs 168 frame gap), plausibly because hold shifts / crossover keep policies reactive. I did not test that explanation separately.
4. Operator credit ended near 0 everywhere (credits decay once the population plateaus), so the market was effectively uniform late in the run; I did not ablate the market (`--nomarket` exists) nor `--pool all` (555 library policies). Single configuration, three seeds, limited tuning: treat the gap to hybrid as an upper bound of what this operator set achieves, not of the idea.
5. Cost: one rollout is a policy interpretation (sense layer), about 40-50k emu frames/s/thread under load, slower than raw pads; frames are counted exactly, wall time is not compared.
